# The Music track arrives WITH its amix: one wrap after everything, output-anchored clips, and a selection-routed cut grammar

Plan #6 (`feature-implementation-plan.md`: import music; drag, trim, split and delete music; support multiple clips; future-ready for volume keyframes, fades, crossfades, multiple audio tracks), queue-jumped ahead of title-gen by the operator (2026-07-13). This is the arc ADR 0066 decision 1 pre-registered: a track arrives WITH its render machinery — the amix IS this slice, so **Music 1** may finally appear. The operator ran it "automatic"; this ADR pre-registers the decisions BEFORE the build, on the ADR 0066 shell + the ADR 0067 output-time timeline. Their one design question this round, in their own words: **"how do i cut the music if my razor cannot cut it?"** — answered by decision 5 (the CapCut answer: select the clip, the verbs act on it).

## Decisions

1. **One amix, wrapped around the FINISHED graph — after the burn AND after the intro.** `yc_render::mix_music(graph, clips, input_base)` is the same move as `prepend_intro` one layer further out: when the graph already terminates in `[aout]` (razor audio, or the intro wrap) that label renames to `[premix]`; otherwise the main audio joins raw from `[0:a]` (the `-ss` seek applies to input 0, so its clock is already the clip's). Every branch — main included — passes `aformat` (fltp / 48 kHz / stereo) so the mix negotiates nothing; each music clip contributes `atrim=in:out, asetpts=PTS-STARTPTS, volume=g, aformat, adelay=<pos_ms>:all=1`, and one `amix=inputs=N+1:duration=first:normalize=0[aout]` re-terminates. `duration=first` + the output `-t` bound keep the output exactly the video's length — music never extends a Short. The ASS burn is untouched by construction (`subtitles=` count stays 1); with music the audio ALWAYS maps from the graph. Music files ride as extra `-i` inputs AFTER the optional intro image, so `input_base` = 1 without an intro, 2 with — pinned in tests. `music: []` leaves graph and args BYTE-IDENTICAL to today.
2. **Schema: `yc_core::MusicClip { path, at_s, in_s, out_s, gain }`; `Clip.music: Vec<MusicClip>` serde-default.** `at_s` anchors the clip on the OUTPUT timeline (it may sit over the thumbnail intro); `in_s..out_s` is the source trim inside the music file; `gain` (default 1.0) is one constant — volume keyframes later sit beside it, fades become `afade` on clip edges, crossfade is the deliberate overlap verb, and multiple audio tracks are the ADR 0066 decision-6 `Vec<Track>` promotion (NOT now: the operator still can't add/remove/reorder tracks, so named statics stay honest). Future-ready = additive. The music is REFERENCED, not copied (the ADR 0067 rule): missing at render = fail loudly with the path.
3. **Two clocks, one honest conversion.** The strip's output clock is `intro + full clip` (razor-removed spans stay visible, veiled — ADR 0065); the EXPORT's clock is `intro + kept spans` (the concat compresses). `at_s` lives in the STRIP's clock — the position the operator placed against the content they saw — and razor edits NEVER rewrite MusicClip data (the razor cannot touch music; that is WHY music is output-anchored, not a caption-style source artifact). At spec time one pure function (`export_clock`: identity over the intro and when nothing is removed; source time compressed through the kept spans past it) converts `at_s` to the export's clock, so the burn plays music against exactly the content the strip showed it over — the gate bar below. When no segment is removed the two clocks coincide and the conversion is the identity, byte-for-byte.
4. **Timeline: Music 1 appears when ≥ 1 clip exists** (the Speakers-zone precedent — an empty lane is dead space); the `+ Music` button lives in the transport always, beside `+ Thumbnail` (rfd picker; mp3/wav/flac/ogg/m4a — whatever the pinned ffmpeg decodes; an undecodable pick is refused BEFORE it can sink a render). Blocks drag in output time and trim at their edges (clamped against neighbors, `in_s ≥ 0`, `out_s ≤` the decoded duration, and the block never leaves `[0 .. out_dur]`); right-click offers **Split here** (two source-continuous clips at the click), **Delete**, and the **gain slider** (one constant, 0–200% — the keyframe arc replaces this control, no new panel). **No overlap on the one track** — drags and trims clamp against neighbors; crossfades arrive WITH overlap semantics later, never as an accident now. Magnet snapping and the pointer-in-TIME drag model mirror the caption blocks.
5. **The selected-clip cut grammar** (the operator's razor question): clicking a music block SELECTS it, and while one is selected the transport verbs act on IT — ✂⏴ trims the selected clip's left edge to the playhead, ⏵✂ its right, Delete removes it — with tooltips saying so; selection also lifts the verbs' intro-refusal (music may sit over the intro; the MAIN razor still refuses there). Nothing selected = the verbs act on the main timeline exactly as today, byte-identical — and the main razor NEVER cuts, moves, or splits music. Esc or clicking empty strip deselects (Esc deselects BEFORE it closes the editor). ⏷ Mark stays a main-timeline verb.
6. **Per-kind honest toggles: Music gets mute + lock, NO eye** (nothing visual). Mute is real THIS arc — the first 🔇 whose OFF state is true: a muted track empties the spec's music (the ADR 0066 eye pattern: the spec's own vocabulary says "mix none") AND silences the preview cues together. Lock ignores the track's timeline gestures. Video 1's mute STAYS deferred to the mixer arc (#7) — not scope-creeped in. The header card takes a new theme accent (`theme::MUSIC`, CapCut's audio-teal family — the operator's eye decides).
7. **Preview: decode at pick, cue on restart.** On pick, the file probes through a new audio-tolerant ffprobe variant (`probe_audio` → duration/sample-rate/channels; the pure parser is unit-tested and `parse_probe` stays pinned to FAIL on audio-only files) and decodes ONCE to native-rate s16 PCM through the pinned ffmpeg (`-f s16le` — the ADR 0067 no-image-crate rule, audio edition: NO codec crates), cached per path. Every `restart_playback(out_t)` — the ONE seek/scrub/jump contract — computes the cues intersecting the export clock from `out_t` and returns them in `EditorAction::Play { voice, music }`: per cue a slice offset into the cached PCM, a `Source::delay` for a clip that hasn't started yet, and the gain. The app spawns one rodio sink per cue (tracked with its gain), sets `prefs.volume × gain`, clears them ALL in `stop_audio`, and re-fires on the same restarts the voice already uses (the video_aligned re-anchor and the intro crossing restart music at the recomputed offset — never two sinks over each other). Parked inside the intro the voice stays silent but music placed there PLAYS — `Play { voice: None, music }`.
8. **`RenderSpec` and `Job::Render` gain `music: Vec<MusicClip>`** (export-clock `at_s`, mute already applied); headless/batch pass `Vec::new()` — untouched, test-pinned. The export modal grows a "Music · N clips" row. `do_render` verifies every music path exists (fail loudly), wraps the finished graph LAST (`mix_music` after `prepend_intro`), threads the paths as inputs, and persists the burned clips on the Clip record (`Clip.music`; the editor does not load project.json back yet — when a project-load arc arrives, revisit which clock persists).

## Considered and rejected

- **Anchoring `at_s` in the EXPORT's compressed clock directly** (adelay = stored value, no conversion) — with razor-removed spans the burn would slide music EARLIER than the strip showed it against the content the operator placed it over; the gate bar ("music enters exactly where the strip shows it") is unmeetable. The conversion is ~10 pure lines, tested.
- **Source-anchoring music like captions (remap through cuts, razor splits it)** — the operator's razor question was answered the CapCut way precisely because music is NOT a source artifact; the main razor removing video time must never secretly chop the music.
- **A `Vec<Track>` model / a second music track now** — pretend-generality (ADR 0066 decision 6 names the promotion point: when the operator can add/remove/reorder tracks).
- **Streaming music decode per restart (ffmpeg spawn per scrub)** — a 100–500 ms hitch on every scrub-release; the decode-once PCM cache makes restarts instant, exactly like the analysis.wav slice the voice already uses. Memory (~10 MB/min stereo 44.1 kHz) is nothing for a solo tool.
- **An `eye` on the Music track** — nothing visual to hide; mute is the honest verb (ADR 0066 per-kind honesty).
- **Waveforms on the blocks** — real value, real cost; nothing in this arc's bars needs it. A later polish round can add it.
- **Auto-ducking / normalize=1** — amix's per-input scaling would change the voice level the moment music appears; `normalize=0` keeps today's voice byte-level untouched and gain stays the operator's slider.

## Bars (pre-registered before the build)

- Filtergraph: ONE amix, applied after the burn AND after the intro wrap; `subtitles=` count stays 1; intro + razor + camera + music compose in one graph; input indices correct with and without an intro; `music: []` → graph and args BYTE-IDENTICAL to today; `adelay` positions in ms; `duration=first:normalize=0` pinned.
- Editor: razor edits never rewrite MusicClip data; trims clamp against neighbors and the decoded duration; split produces two source-continuous clips; mute empties the spec's music; the spec carries music only when clips exist; `export_clock` identity without cuts, compressed with them, intro region untouched; the cut verbs ROUTE by selection (music selected → they edit that clip; none → the main razor, byte-identical to today).
- Probe: the audio-duration parser unit-tested pure (an mp3 with no video stream must probe; `parse_probe` is pinned to fail there).
- The burn gate (the operator's EAR, on a real export): music enters exactly where the strip shows it (over the intro if placed there), trims/splits land, gain audible as set, speech still legible, output ends with the video. Gate on the burn, not the preview.
