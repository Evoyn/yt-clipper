# The timeline razor cuts time out of the export; captions become unit blocks; the camera plan takes the operator's own framing

Same-day follow-through on ADR 0064: the operator drove the first build and ruled on it (2026-07-12) — "split" read as a clip cut but only changed framing; there was no way to cut TIME at all; dragging a caption made neighbours vanish from the timeline; caption display duration had no control; and framing in Active Speaker was face-click-only ("i cant add cut my own camera frame"). This ADR records the second slice: the razor (plan #3 as the operator actually meant it), the caption-block model fix, per-shot manual framing, motion presets (#9), and saved caption presets (#12).

## Decisions

1. **Two cut verbs, named for what they do.** `✂ Cut clip` razors the TIMELINE (segments; removable spans); `◆ Camera cut` splits a camera-plan shot (framing changes, no time removed). Distinct glyphs and colours on the strip: white ✂ full-height markers for razor cuts, gold ◆ markers for camera cuts.
2. **The razor removes spans from the export — honestly.** `RazorState` (interior cuts + a removed flag per segment; the last kept segment is unremovable) feeds `RenderSpec.keep` (kept spans; `None` when nothing is removed — razor cuts alone are markers). The render (ADR 0065 wiring): the camera plan — or the static layout promoted to one shot per kept span — is intersected with the kept spans **in source time** (`CameraPlan::cut_to`, glide pieces sample `layout_at` at their bounds so motion is identical), the filtergraph gains per-shot **`atrim` + `concat v=1:a=1`** (video and audio cut as pairs — A/V cannot drift), and captions remap onto the compressed output timeline (`remap_units_through_cuts`) **through the spans the plan actually kept** (`kept_spans`), so a dropped sub-frame sliver can never desync text from audio. Preview playback skips removed spans by restarting at each kept span (the same contract as a scrub). Headless/batch renders pass `keep: None` — untouched.
3. **Caption timeline blocks are UNITS, not burn lines.** Line blocks merged/regrouped live while their units moved — the operator's "dragging erases the other caption" bug: nothing was deleted, the line model had honestly regrouped, but stable panel rows vanishing from the strip reads as data loss. One block per `CaptionUnit` (= one panel row) is a stable object: body drags, edges trim, click selects, right-click deletes. The burn's line grouping stays visible as a thin **rail** under the blocks (grouping genres only); dragging the rail moves the whole line. The on-video caption overlay keeps the line model — the burn stays ground truth (ADR 0036).
4. **Caption display duration is the unit's end**, editable in both places: the timeline block's right edge and a new end-time field per panel row (start still MOVES, preserving duration). The one-line-at-a-time clamp at burn time is presentation, deliberately not overridable.
5. **Per-shot manual framing in Active Speaker**: dragging the crop box (Original view) or panning/zooming the output (Preview view) reframes THE SHOT under the displayed frame — `display_time`, never the playhead clock — and drops that shot's follow (`pan_to = None`, the click_face contract). The camera plan is the operator's to reframe, not only to retarget. Every plan edit re-audits.
6. **Motion presets (#9) are camera plans**, not new render machinery: Zoom in/out, Pan left/right, Ken Burns (glides via `pan_to`) and Punch in (a hard two-shot cut at 55%) synthesized over the operator's manual full-frame crop; preview via `layout_at`, export via `camera.fg`. Stacked framing has no single crop to glide — presets hide there. Manual-with-motion is a deliberate mode: the speaker analysis no longer auto-arms Active Speaker over it.
7. **Saved caption presets (#12) live in `workspace/settings.json`** (`AppSettings` now serde-derived: volume + named `UserCaptionPreset`s), applied/deleted from the Caption panel, updated in place on same-name save. Same debounced-write + exit-flush persistence as the volume.

## Considered and rejected

- **Razor via a new dedicated filtergraph builder** — the camera concat already is a per-span trim/concat; adding audio pairs to it is one flag. Two builders would drift.
- **Remapping captions through the editor's keep list directly** — the plan may drop sliver pieces the keep list still counts; remapping through the plan's own `kept_spans` makes video/audio/text provably share one time base.
- **Keeping line blocks + preventing regroup during drags** — fights the burn model; unit blocks + the rail show both truths at once.
- **Free per-shot crops in Preview for split-screen (group) shots** — a two-panel shot has no single crop; face-click retargeting still covers it.

## Amendment — the caption lanes (operator round-3 verdict, same day)

The operator drove the round-2 build: captions still fought each other (stuck at neighbours; a time-overlap silently hid one at burn), the panel had become cramped to the point of uneditable on a narrow panel, and editing a time re-sorted the list mid-edit so the row swapped under the cursor ("it replaced the other caption"). And they asked for the CapCut shape outright: a **dedicated track for added captions**. Decisions:

1. **Two caption lanes.** The pipeline's transcript (row "auto") and the operator's own captions (row "yours" — `manual_units`, a pure editor artifact). `＋ Caption` adds to the manual lane. The burn merges both, start-sorted, through the transcript override (`merged_transcript`) — the Dialect store remains untouched, and a late worker caption echo can never displace the operator's lane.
2. **Captions drag freely** — past each other too. Order clamps are gone; each lane sorts on gesture release (never mid-gesture: pointer-tracked indices stay stable) and the panel sorts only when no time widget is focused/dragged (the mid-edit row swap was the "replaced the other caption" bug).
3. **No silent swallowing.** A caption the burn would hide (huge-word: the next merged caption starts at/before it — its display clamps to zero) is flagged **red** with a tooltip naming the fix, instead of vanishing. The clamp itself stays: one line on screen at a time is the burn's grammar; the editor's job is to make the consequence visible, not to reorder the operator's times behind their back.
4. **Panel rows are two-line**: times + actions above, the text field **full-width** below — a narrow panel can no longer squeeze the text into uneditability.
5. The burn-line **rail** now spans both lanes (it reads the merged order via `merged_caption_refs`, whose construction provably matches `merged_transcript`); dragging it moves the whole on-screen line across lanes.

## Amendment 2 — CapCut razor verbs; the video track (operator round-4 verdict, same day)

1. ~~The operator's caption OVERWRITES an overlapping auto caption~~ — built as suppression, then **superseded the same day by Amendment 3**: the operator's real want was two captions ON SCREEN at once ("why can't captions share time although they're on separate tracks?"). See Amendment 3.
2. **CapCut-simple razor verbs**: `✂ Cut` (split at playhead), `← Left` / `Right →` (remove everything left/right of the playhead — ensures a cut there, reusing one within the min-segment radius), `✕ Remove` (drop the segment under the playhead; also the **Delete** key). "Cut the middle" = cut twice, park inside, ✕. Right-click restore unchanged; the last-kept-segment guard applies to every verb (`remove_side` refuses a removal that would empty the export).
3. **The video track**: the clip's full length as filmstrip thumbnails (the frames Prepare already extracts) drawn between the ruler and the caption lanes — "so we know where to cut". Scrubbing works through it (it is strip surface); razor veils cover it, so removed spans visibly dim their video content.

## Amendment 3 — the operator's captions are a second, SIMULTANEOUS render stream (operator round-5 question, same day)

"So I can't have 2 captions at the same time?" — they can, once the manual lane stops being merged into the auto stream and becomes its own: `generate_ass` gains a `manual: &[CaptionUnit]` stream, burned as **layer-1 Dialogue events at their own `\pos` one block above the auto anchor** (`resolve_manual_placement`, shared with the editor overlay per ADR 0036 — one geometry). Explicit positioning turns libass collision-shifting off, so the anchor is the contract; text uppercases and pops like every caption; timing is exactly as placed (zero-duration floor only — no clamps against the auto stream, whose events are byte-identical with or without manual captions, test-pinned). `RenderSpec`/`Job::Render` carry `manual_captions` beside the override (never inside it, never into a store); the razor remaps both streams through the same kept spans. The editor preview draws the active manual caption WITH the auto line. Suppression, ghosting, and the merged-transcript machinery are deleted; collision warnings are now per-lane only (auto huge-word zero-display; two manual captions overprinting each other). Within-lane, one-at-a-time remains the auto grammar; the manual lane is the operator's, warned but never auto-resolved.

## Amendment 4 — per-caption placement; the marker-scissors razor (operator round-6 verdict, same day)

1. **Each manual caption is draggable anywhere on the canvas.** `ManualCaption { unit, placement }` (yc-core) carries a per-caption `CaptionPlacement`: `None` = the default raised anchor, `Some` = the operator's drag (burned via its own `\pos` + `\fs` for their per-caption scale — test-pinned). The editor pairs `manual_units` with `manual_places` (every structural edit goes through shared insert/remove/sort helpers so the pairing cannot skew); the preview draws EVERY active manual caption at its own anchor, each drag/scroll/right-click-resettable. The overprint warning now requires shared time AND shared space (both captions at the default anchor) — dragged-apart captions overlap freely.
2. **The razor collapsed to marker-scissors** (the operator's flow: "place middle marker... click cut left, it cuts until it touches the marker"): `▼ Mark` places a draggable reference point; `✂←` removes from the playhead back to the marker (clip start when none on the left), `→✂` forward to the marker (clip end). Both ride one primitive, `RazorState::remove_span(a, b)` — boundary cuts ensured (near-cut reuse), fully-covered segments removed, the last-kept guard intact. The four round-4 buttons are gone; Delete (segment under playhead) and the right-click menu (cut here / marker here / remove / restore) remain.

## Amendment 5 — polish round (operator verdict 2026-07-13: "cut and captions is good")

The scissors trio is one merged segmented control (zero gap, shared end corners) with glyphs that actually exist in the font stack — the round-6 ← ▼ → ◆ ＋ all rendered as tofu (only egui's emoji fonts back the UI font; ⏴⏵⏷ live in the same font as the proven ⏸, and the strip's marker head + camera badges are now PAINTED shapes, font-proof). Double-clicking any caption on the preview — auto line or the operator's own — focuses its text field in the panel (the word under the playhead for a multi-word line). The **Camera-cut transport button is parked** until the keyframe arc gives per-cut framing real function (operator call; the strip's right-click menu keeps the verb for Active-Speaker clips, and the plan's markers/reframe/audit machinery is untouched).

## Validation

111 tests green across the touched crates (round 4: remove-span guards + cut reuse; round 5: the simultaneous manual stream — layer/anchor/uppercase/floor pinned, auto events proven untouched by an overlapping manual caption; round 6: per-caption `\pos`+`\fs` placement, marker-scissors semantics incl. the middle-cut flow and wrong-side fallback) (new: razor add/merge/toggle/kept-spans/skip, `cut_to` span+glide+sliver, caption remap drop/shift/clamp, razor filtergraph audio pairing + `[aout]` mapping, motion plan coverage/direction, settings round-trip with presets). `cargo check` clean on default and `face,align,ser`. The feel gates — razor drag, per-shot reframe, a cut export's A/V/caption sync, and the two-lane caption feel — are the operator's eye on the release build and a real razored export (gate on the burn).
