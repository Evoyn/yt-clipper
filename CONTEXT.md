# YT Clipper

A Windows-first desktop app that turns long VODs — gaming streams and podcasts — into vertical short-form clips: transcription, moment detection, framing, speaker tracking, captioning, and export all happen on the user's own machine.

## Language

**Creator**:
A streamer whose VODs the operator clips with their permission. A Creator carries saved defaults (facecam layout, caption style, spoken language) that apply to every VOD of theirs.
_Avoid_: channel, streamer, user

**VOD**:
The long source recording a project is built around — a finished stream recording on YouTube, or a local video file. Always belongs to a Creator.
_Avoid_: stream, video, source

**Moment**:
A scored candidate time range within a VOD, surfaced by analysis (or marked manually by the operator), awaiting review. A detected Moment starts and ends on sentence boundaries, aiming for 45–180 s of real speech (shorter when speech runs dry — ADR 0040); a manually-marked Moment keeps its verbatim range, untouched.
_Avoid_: highlight, candidate, event

**Signal**:
One strand of evidence behind a Moment's score — chat-rate, loudness, lexicon, arousal, or LLM judgment — kept separate (never blended away) so the ranking can be retuned and the operator can see *why* a Moment surfaced.
_Avoid_: feature, metric, factor

**Arousal**:
The Signal measuring the emotional *activation* of the streamer's voice — how worked-up they sound — from a speech-emotion model's arousal axis. Deliberately ignores *which* emotion: a laugh, a rage, and a hype-moment all score high (all clip-worthy). Its job is to tell an emotional reaction apart from merely loud audio (a game explosion, music, a cutscene) that loudness alone cannot.
_Avoid_: sentiment / valence (positive-vs-negative — explicitly not measured), excitement (reserved for the lexicon), emotion (too broad — implies classifying which emotion)

**LLM judgment**:
The Signal from a local language model reading a Moment's transcript: a judgment of how clip-worthy the *streamer's* speech is, scored per-candidate and kept relative to the others. Unlike the lexicon (which counts excitement words), it weighs meaning in context — and it is explicitly told to discount scripted in-game dialogue and cutscene narration, so a dramatic game line is not clip-worthy unless the streamer reacts to it. Runs locally on the GPU after transcription unloads; no cloud.
_Avoid_: rerank (it is a Signal in the ensemble, not a post-sort stage), GPT / cloud (it is a local GGUF), sentiment (it judges clip-worthiness, not positive-vs-negative)

**Clip**:
A Moment the operator has promoted for production. A Clip gets framing, captions, and an export; a Moment that is never promoted gets nothing.
_Avoid_: short, video, segment

**Title**:
A catchy ≤60-character short-form title generated for a Moment by the LLM judge at detect time (it already reads the transcript — ADR 0010/0015), stored on the Moment and used to name the rendered Short (`<title>.mp4`) so a re-promote never overwrites a previous one. A manually-marked Moment has no generated title; the render then names the Short by timestamp (`clip-<m-ss>`). Distinct from the VOD's own `title` (the stream's name, which names the **stream folder**).
_Avoid_: caption (that is the on-screen burned text), filename, label

**Stream folder**:
A VOD's output directory, `workspace/<creator>/<stream-title>/` (sanitized from VOD metadata — ADR 0015). The rendered Shorts (named by their generated Title) sit at its root; every intermediate (analysis audio, downloaded Segments, `project.json`, captions, font) lives under its `data/` subfolder. It realizes Creator scoping on disk; the **Creator store** (`workspace/creators.json`) holds the matching per-Creator defaults.
_Avoid_: workspace (that is the parent holding every stream folder), project folder

**Creator store**:
The global `workspace/creators.json` (`core::CreatorStore`) of per-Creator remembered defaults, keyed by Creator name (ADR 0016) — the long-deferred `creators.json`. Today it remembers a Creator's **Caption Style**, **language**, and **Caption engine**: importing a known Creator's VOD seeds the render's genre/engine to their last-used ones, and an **Auto**-language import (the GUI/CLI default since 2026-07-02) applies the Creator's saved language — each render saves them back (operator overrides always win; explicit `en|id|ja` beats Auto; `YC_QWEN_ENS` overrides the engine per-invocation without being remembered). Seam/crop defaults are reserved for a later Creator-aware-framing slice.
_Avoid_: project.json (that is per-VOD, under the stream folder's `data/`), dialect store (that is per-language transcription corrections)

**Segment**:
The padded span of full-quality VOD video downloaded for a Clip when it is promoted — bounded by keyframes, so always a little longer than the Clip's range. The frame-accurate export is cut from the Segment; the VOD's video is never downloaded whole (ADR 0001, ADR 0006).
_Avoid_: clip, chunk, section, source

**Vocal stem**:
The streamer's voice track separated from a Segment's audio — music and game SFX stripped out by a source-separation model. It isolates *voice from music/SFX*, **not one speaker from another**: an in-game NPC or cutscene voice survives it (telling those apart is a separate, future concern). Produced per-Clip behind the `sep` feature. **Rejected for captions (ADR 0014)**: htdemucs is a music separator and drops real spoken speech, so the export captions the mixed audio; the validated runtime is kept off-by-default for a possible future arousal-discovery use.
_Avoid_: mic track / mic (it is not literally the streamer's microphone feed — any voice in the mix survives), denoised audio (it removes music/SFX, not just background noise)

**Cleaned voice**:
The caption-input audio after a gentle speech **denoiser** (DeepFilterNet, the `enh` feature) suppresses game SFX / music while *keeping* the streamer's voice — fed to whisper so it reads the voice above the noise on a masked Clip. Distinct from the **Vocal stem**: that *separates* voice from music and can drop quiet speech (ADR 0014); this *denoises* with an attenuation limit, so it never gates speech down to the dead silence whisper hallucinates into (ADR 0029). Caption-input only — the rendered Clip's audible audio is always the mix.
_Avoid_: vocal stem (that is separation), noise gate (it is a learned denoiser, not a threshold gate), enhanced audio (the Clip's audio is unchanged — only whisper's transcription input is)

**Dialect store**:
A curatable, **layered** set of per-language files (the `DialectLexicon`) that lifts the caption-transcription ceiling for a Creator's accent / slang / viewer names — the *linguistic* error that no audio processing fixes (ADR 0014). **Three layers merge per render, most-specific winning** (ADR 0031): the bundled **base** (`assets/dialect/<lang>.json` — the shared real-word dictionary + generic fixes + config), a **per-Creator** store (`workspace/<creator>/<lang>.json` — a streamer's confirmed slang/names, applied across all their VODs), and a **per-clip** store (`<stream>/<ClipStem>.<lang>.json`, beside the exported Short — *that clip's* auto-harvest, for easy per-export curation). It drives a deterministic `wrong → right` correction dict (always on); the **LLM correction pass** for context-sensitive overrides a global dict can't do safely (ADR 0030); opt-in whisper priming (`prime`, off — it drifts); and **auto-harvest**: words whisper was unsure of that aren't in the bundled real-word dictionary are appended as `unverified` to-dos to the **per-clip** store each caption run, so the operator's review queue self-populates per export. Each harvested entry's note records **where the word came from** — the generated Short Title + the absolute VOD timestamp (ADR 0022). A confirmed (filled `right`) per-clip entry **auto-promotes** to the per-Creator store on the next render, so curating once in the small per-clip file makes it durable for the Creator (ADR 0031) — unless it is marked **`clip_only`**, a fix scoped to a single clip (a real word meant as slang in just that clip) that must not globalize. The harvest dictionary always loads a bundled common-given-names list (`names.words.txt`, Indonesian + international — ADR 0023) so a correctly-read **common** name isn't flagged; an **unusual** viewer handle still harvests. Distinct from the per-Creator `names.json` roster (a streamer's actual viewer handles + garble→name corrections).
_Avoid_: lexicon (overloaded — that is the excitement-word list for detection scoring), dictionary (that is the bundled real-word wordlist + common-names list the harvest filters against, not the corrections)

**Time pin**:
An `at_s`-anchored Dialect-store correction — the operator's ear as ground truth for **where** a word belongs, not just what it is (ADR 0051). It runs after all automated timing (fusion or forced alignment) and moves the occurrence of its word nearest the pinned VOD moment onto a measured speech onset, skipping rather than guessing when nothing plausible is near. `wrong == right` is a pure re-time (the GUE class — a duplicated common word the aligner grabs at the wrong copy, ADR 0055); a differing `right` renames in place at the moment (the GEMES twin fix, safe only once the recall admission stopped the token floating — ADR 0056). Engine-parity: whisper renders run the same positional pass (ADR 0051). Nearly always `clip_only` — a pin encodes one clip's moment, not Creator language.
_Avoid_: pin (unqualified — also used for pinned download sources), anchor (that is the segment-seek audio lock), re-time (the Transcript override never re-times; a pin re-times exactly one word)

**Recall admission**:
The ensemble's post-vote re-admission of a word the strict-majority vote dropped over speechful audio (ADR 0056): a distinctive word (long enough, not a common function word) that whisper — the witness — carries more occurrences of than the merged vote does is inserted **text-only** at its witness position, at most one per fuzzy family per vote. Three structural guards, each earned by a live false positive: no common/short words, no disagreement zones (whisper re-spelling a word the vote already carries is Dialect-store curation, not a hole), no range edges (the clip boundary cuts words into stumps only whisper transcribes). Time-blind at admission (ADR 0050's lesson); its standing bar is **zero words admitted on turn-taking controls**. It exists because denoising suppresses an overlapping co-speaker as noise — so cross-denoise agreement is anti-correlated with hearing exactly the words the vote loses (ADR 0056 measured the ADR 0052 clause dead twice).
_Avoid_: recovery (implies re-decoding audio; admission is text-only over already-decoded streams), hole fill (the symptom it fixes, not the mechanism), transcript override (the operator's words verbatim; admission operates on whisper's)

**Review queue**:
A Creator's harvested caption to-dos awaiting curation — the `unverified` entries (blank `right`) in their Dialect store, each a word whisper misheard that the operator has not yet corrected. Surfaced in the app's detail pane (ADR 0032), grouped by the clip they were harvested from, so the operator fills the correct word in-app — hearing it in context via a VOD jump link (ADR 0022) — instead of hand-editing JSON. Filling an entry's `right` and saving removes it from the queue (it becomes a confirmed correction that applies to all the Creator's future clips). Reads and writes the **per-Creator** layer directly, the same layer auto-promote targets.
_Avoid_: to-do list / backlog (too generic), harvest (that is the act of populating the queue, not the queue itself)

**Layout**:
The arrangement of a Clip's 1080×1920 canvas. Two variants: stacked (gameplay Panel above facecam Panel, divided by the Seam) and full-frame (a single Panel filling the canvas — the gameplay, or the Facecam alone during a talking-session moment where the streamer's cam is the content). Chosen per Clip by M6 auto-detect (ADR 0011) unless the operator's **Layout preference** forces a specific one.
_Avoid_: composite, template, frame

**Layout preference**:
The operator's explicit choice of which Layout to frame a Clip with (ADR 0017), overriding M6 auto-detect: _auto_ (the ADR 0011 three-way decision), or a forced _stacked_ / _full cam_ / _full gameplay_. A forced choice still uses the detected Facecam Crop when one is found, else a sensible seed. A global session/per-invocation setting (GUI top-bar menu + a `--batch`/`--headless` CLI token), defaulting to _auto_; persisting it per-Creator is a later Creator-aware-framing slice (ADR 0016). It exists because the operator's preferred stacked framing must be selectable in `--batch`, which never opens the nudge editor.
_Avoid_: layout (that is the realized arrangement; this is the operator's pick of it), template

**Facecam**:
The region of the source video showing the streamer's webcam overlay. Its location and size are not fixed across a VOD — a small corner inset during gameplay, but the streamer may switch to a full-screen cam during a talking session — so it is detected per Clip from the promoted Segment's frames, not assumed (M6, ADR 0011). A cam can hold **more than one face** (a 2-person co-stream): all persistent faces are detected and the stacked facecam Panel frames their **union**, so neither person is cropped out (ADR 0026). The detected Facecam seeds the facecam Panel's Crop in a stacked Layout, or the whole canvas in a full-frame talking-session Layout.
_Avoid_: webcam (the hardware), facecam Panel (that is the canvas region it is shown in, not the source region)

**Panel**:
A region of the canvas that displays exactly one Crop of the VOD, filled edge-to-edge — never letterboxed, never stretched.
_Avoid_: slot, zone, view

**Seam**:
The horizontal boundary between the gameplay and facecam Panels in a stacked Layout. Its position is per-Clip, defaulted from the Creator.
_Avoid_: split, divider

**Crop**:
The rectangle of source-video pixels a Panel displays. Aspect-locked to its Panel: resizing zooms, dragging pans.
_Avoid_: selection, region, window

**Caption Style**:
A named preset describing how captions look and animate (font, colors, outline, animation genre — rolling-pop, huge-word, karaoke). Saved per Creator, overridable per Clip. The karaoke genre (enum `KaraokeFill`) highlights **per word as a whole, snapping** to the accent colour at each word's spoken onset (ASS `\k`, cumulative — ADR 0018), not a left-to-right fill.
_Avoid_: theme, template, skin

**Caption placement**:
The per-Clip override of where the caption block sits on the canvas and how large its text draws — set by dragging/resizing the captions over the editor's preview playback (ADR 0036). Absent (the default, and always in headless), captions keep the built-in anchor and the Caption Style's size. Presentation data on the Clip — never curation: a placement says nothing about words.
_Avoid_: caption position (underspecifies — placement also scales), caption style (that is the shared look/animation preset; placement is one Clip's geometry)

**Caption engine**:
The per-Creator choice of how a Clip's caption words are transcribed: **Whisper** (single-decode) or the **Qwen ensemble** (a five-variant vote with whisper as a voter — ADR 0034; timing from wav2vec2-CTC forced alignment on production `align` builds since ADR 0055, with the whisper-DTW fusion as the fallback skeleton and `YC_FORCED_ALIGN=0` as the off-switch). A **new** Creator starts on the ensemble (operator ruling, ADR 0061); an existing Creator keeps their saved engine — a never-flipped record still reads Whisper, and flipping it stays a deliberate act. A closed enum, not a model picker — the ensemble is a measured recipe, and a new model earns entry only through ADR 0034's gate. Remembered in the Creator store like Caption Style (ADR 0016); switching it for a curated Creator is a deliberate act (ADR 0033): the app states which of their existing corrections carry across (ADR 0035).
_Avoid_: model picker (a recipe, not a GGUF choice), decoder (ambiguous with whisper's internal decode config), ASR toggle

**Studio**:
The full-window editor page a Promote opens into (ADR 0039): transcript editor left, Before/After video preview center (Original = source frame + crop tools + face overlays; Preview = the composited 9:16 output + captions + safe-area guide), properties right (Camera / Framing incl. Motion presets / Caption style incl. the operator's saved presets), timeline bottom — since plan #13 (ADR 0066) a multi-Track strip in the Premiere/CapCut shape: a header column (Track names + painted eye/lock toggles, a zoom corner), then ruler, video filmstrip, the two caption Tracks, speaker lanes plus the Voice lane's row, cut markers, playhead, and a scrollbar row, all through one zoom/scroll viewport (ctrl+wheel zooms about the pointer, wheel scrolls, snap tolerances stay in screen points). The timeline is directly editable (ADR 0064/0065): a **video track** (the clip's full length as filmstrip thumbnails) sits under the ruler so the operator can see what they're cutting; captions live on **two lanes** below it — the pipeline's transcript ("auto") and the operator's own added captions ("yours", a pure editor artifact) — one block per caption, dragging freely in time (lanes sort on release), trimming at the edges; the two lanes are two **simultaneous render streams**: the operator's captions burn as their own events — **each draggable anywhere on the canvas** (drag on the preview, scroll to resize, right-click to reset; default = one block above the auto anchor) — so both streams CAN share time on screen (warnings fire only within a lane: an auto caption its successor would zero out, or two of the operator's captions sharing both time and the default spot), with the auto stream's line grouping drawn as a rail beneath; **two cut verbs**, named for what they do: a **Camera cut** (gold ◆) splits a camera-plan shot so the framing changes (drag to move, right-click to delete, drag the picture to reframe the shot — the operator's own per-shot framing), and the **timeline razor** (white ✂, marker-scissors: `▼ Mark` places the reference point, `✂←` cuts from the playhead back to it — or the clip start — and `→✂` forward to it — or the clip end; Delete drops the segment under the playhead) which removes spans from the export outright — video, audio, and captions together, preview playback skipping them. All gestures snap to the playhead (and cuts to the source frame grid) and flow through the Transcript override, the operator-overridable Camera plan, and the razor's kept spans, so the render burns exactly what the timeline shows. The transport also carries the master playback volume (0–200 %, persisted in `workspace/settings.json` — playback only, never the export). Prepare auto-queues the caption pre-pass (`Job::Transcribe`) so the transcript is editable before any render; Export shows a summary (length / resolution / captions / camera / timeline cuts / estimated time) before rendering.
_Avoid_: nudge editor (the old docked panel it replaced), preview window (it is a page, not a popup), split (ambiguous — say Camera cut for framing, Clip cut for the razor)

**Track**:
One horizontal band of the Studio timeline with a header (name + painted eye/lock toggles) and its own content — today: Video 1 · Main (the filmstrip), Captions · auto (the pipeline's stream), Captions · yours (the operator's stream), and the Speakers analysis zone (ADR 0066). The **eye** is output visibility: the stream leaves the preview overlay AND the burn together (the preview cannot drift from the burn), while its blocks stay on the timeline, dimmed and editable. The **lock** ignores that track's timeline gestures; the caption panel stays live (it is the deliberate precision surface). Toggles are per-kind honest — only a toggle whose OFF state is real today appears: Video 1 has no eye (hiding the only video would export nothing) and no mute (that arrives with the audio-mixer arc). **An AI artifact and an operator artifact never share a Track** (operator ruling 2026-07-13) — the caption pair is the first embodiment, and every future generated-vs-placed pair splits the same way; a Track arrives WITH its render machinery, never before (no Music/Images/B-roll tracks until their arcs land); Track order is z-order for video-kind Tracks (top = above).
_Avoid_: lane (a Track's drawing area — the caption "lanes" predate their headers), layer (z-order binds only video-kind Tracks), row (any horizontal band, ruler included)

**Camera mode**:
How the Studio frames a Clip: **Manual** (the operator's own crops), **Center**, **Auto face** (a static crop on the most persistent face), **Active Speaker** (the cut-based plan following whoever talks — the podcast recommendation), or **Group** (everyone at once; 2 people = the stacked split screen). The AI modes derive framing from the Speaker analysis; dragging the frame in an AI mode flips to Manual.
_Avoid_: layout preference (that is the pre-editor stacked/full choice, ADR 0017), tracking mode

**Speaker track**:
One tracked **seat** in a podcast Clip (`yc_frame::speaker`): a persistent face position (podcast cameras are static), per-bin **mouth activity** (luma change over a fixed grid in the lower face box), and the "Person A/B/C" label (left-to-right). The **Speaker analysis** (ADR 0038) gates activity by an audio VAD and attributes a speaker per time bin with a switch margin + confirmation hold, giving the editor's face overlays, speaker timeline, and confidence chip. In a source that cuts between camera angles, a track is a screen *position*, not a person — different people can hold one seat across angles (ADR 0042 measured it); person identity is the **Voice lane**'s job.
_Avoid_: diarization (the audio-embedding upgrade is the Voice lane, gated behind ADR 0042 — attribution today remains mouth-motion), face cluster (that is M6's Facecam detection; a track adds time-series activity), person (a track is a seat; the voice is the person)

**Voice lane**:
The speaker-diarization evidence measured beside mouth motion: voiced stretches of a Clip's audio embedded into compact voiceprints and clustered, each cluster one person's *voice* — an identity that survives camera angles and seats. A voice joins **per person over the Occupant map** when the map is available (ADR 0044): its co-occurrence evidence is admitted per camera only when clean — contested stretches where several mouths move are no evidence at all — and a cluster whose clean evidence names one person claims that person's seat in *any* segment the map places them, while a cluster whose evidence names two people is refused an identity outright (its clean camera-local claims survive). A known voice's person **visibly absent** from a fully-known camera marks an **off-screen speaker** — a claim of positive evidence, never of ignorance — which mouth motion cannot detect. Without the map (missing face-id models, or a follow-visible Clip) the join stays seat-scoped per angle, with no off-screen claims. Since its gate passed (ADR 0042, 2026-07-05) the lane runs inside the production Speaker analysis whenever its model is present (missing or broken model = mouth-only analysis, healed from the Diagnostics downloads): the attribution the camera follows is the **fused** lane — the mouth lane's own commitments stand, and a confidently-joined voice that the mouth cannot refute by its own switch margin takes the bin — and the Studio timeline draws the voice's own row beside the seat lanes, off-screen spans marked. It drives the Camera plan's off-screen split screen and its voice-gated interjection cuts.
_Avoid_: diarization (names the technique, not this artifact), Signal (reserved for Moment scoring), voiceprint (that is one window's embedding; the lane is the attributed timeline)

**Occupant map**:
The face re-id evidence of who OCCUPIES each seat, per camera angle: a Speaker track's face crops per angle segment, embedded (landmark-aligned) and clustered across the clip into persons, giving (segment × seat → person) and merging segments into the source's real CAMERAS wherever their occupants match — a face seen only once (a pose extreme) stays an **unknown occupant**, never an identity, and its segment never merges. It exists because a seat is a screen position, not a person (ADR 0042): the map is the de-circularizing anchor for the Voice lane's join — one voice cluster joining different persons in different cameras is measurably impure evidence, which co-occurrence alone can never see. Its instrument gate passed on the operator's eyes (ADR 0043, the contact sheet: every row one human), and since ADR 0044 the production Speaker analysis builds it per attribution-regime Clip (full-res targeted seeks at analyze time; either face-id model missing = seat-scoped join, healed from the Diagnostics downloads) as the ground the person-scoped Voice lane joins over.
_Avoid_: face cluster (that is M6's Facecam detection), identity map (underspecifies — the map is per-seat-per-angle occupancy), person tracking (tracks stay seats; the map annotates them with occupants)

**Shared reaction**:
A stretch of a Clip where the group reacts together — laughter first; applause and cheering are the same class — instead of one person holding the floor. During it nobody on screen is THE speaker, so the Camera plan owes it the visible pair's split screen (the off-screen split's exact "nobody visible is the speaker" semantics, fed by a second honest fuel), never a solo on whoever's mouth moves most. It is its own acoustic evidence class, measured per time bin beside the Voice lane: the speech-domain instruments provably cannot see it (ADR 0044 — contested share, absence share, and voice-cluster composition all failed to discriminate the Deddy laughter stretch, whose voiced time fragments into embedding scraps), so it takes a dedicated audio-event instrument, gated on pre-declared bars like every lane before it (ADR 0045). Since its wiring gate passed (ADR 0046, 2026-07-07) the mask runs inside the production Speaker analysis for attribution-regime Clips whenever the pinned tagger is present (missing tagger = no reaction splits and a Camera-panel note, healed from the Diagnostics downloads; a follow-visible Clip never computes it): a plan piece carrying enough masked time flips to the visible pair's split screen, and the Studio timeline's evidence row paints the mask in gold beside the voice claims.
_Avoid_: laughter (one species of it — the class name must not block applause/cheering later), reaction (too broad — one listener's backchannel is not shared), interjection (one voice cutting in — the opposite of shared), off-screen (different fuel, same grammar)

**Solo presence**:
The verified fact a solo Shot owes before it ships: that what it frames is the attributed person's real, currently-visible face — the subject measured inside the planned crop during the shot, and the measurement itself a real face, not a face-like blob on set dressing. The 2026-07-07 shakedown measured its absence as the tool's worst defect (nine publisher-visible hits across all three clips and both regimes), in two failure classes: a shot with **no measurement** persists a stale crop and the camera audit is structurally blind (no measurement, no finding), and a shot with a **false, angle-stale measurement** frames set dressing while the audit reads "subject in crop" and passes. It is verified per solo shot on **Bar P** — a solo shot flags when it is majority-unmeasured AND the planned crop holds no plausible subject-scale face: too small (a poster/figurine, ≤ 0.35× the subject's reference) or too large (an oversized false detection ~2×, the back-of-head / guitar-body blob — ADR 0048). Since its instrument gate passed (ADR 0047) and its wiring gate passed (ADR 0048, 2026-07-07) the check runs inside the production Speaker analysis for attribution-regime Clips (targeted analyze-time seeks over each low-measured solo crop; follow-visible waits — it builds no Occupant map, so no identity anchor): a failing solo heals into the same grammar as the off-screen and shared-reaction splits — the visible pair's split screen, a held verified crop, or an honest wide, never a solo on an unverified subject — a third honest fuel into that grammar. A split **never shows one person twice** (two panels framing the same source region collapse to the honest wide — operator rule).
_Avoid_: presence (unqualified — a Speaker track's presence % is airtime, a different measurement), face detection (the tracker detects constantly; this verifies what the plan *frames*), occupancy (the Occupant map's per-angle knowledge — one input to this, not the fact itself)

**Camera plan**:
The cut-based dynamic framing of an Active-Speaker Clip: contiguous **Shots**, each a clip-relative time span framed by one Layout (solo 9:16 crop, or a split screen for a group shot). The planner picks one of **two regimes by how many faces are usually on screen** (ADR 0038 refinement, 2026-07-04): a **static wide shot** (everyone in frame) uses audio **attribution** to choose the speaker and cut between fixed positions — and when that "wide" source turns out to be an edit anyway (scene cuts between two-person angles, jump cuts: the Deddy case), each attribution shot **re-frames at the source's own cut frames** (WHO from attribution, WHERE per angle; a sliver piece folds into its neighbour), and a solo piece **reuses its seat's remembered framing** (whole-clip memory, fixed anchors) unless the subject's measured center/size has left a dead-zone since the anchor was set — a jump cut back to the same camera never twitches the crop, while a real angle change still re-frames fully at the cut, where a re-frame is perceptually free. One seat's fragmented tracks (a detection gap + a shift past the match radius mints a duplicate) are **merged back into one track** when near + temporally complementary + each fragment shares the frame with someone outside the pair — that last gate keeps two people's *solo* framings (which show nobody else) from ever fusing (ADR 0038, 2026-07-05); a **multicam edit or solo source** (≈1 face at a time — the source already cut to its subject) instead **mirrors the source's own cuts**, using the source's **actual cut frames** (ffmpeg pixel-level scene detection) as the shot boundaries so every cut lands frame-exact regardless of sample rate — each source shot framed *statically* on its subject's position (correct from the first frame, no jitter), one visible face followed and **two-or-more shown as a split screen**, so the crop is never parked on an off-screen position and a wide shot shows everyone. **Cuts are the grammar between speakers** — a camera hunting for the active speaker reads as amateur. Two Voice-lane behaviors refine that grammar (ADR 0042, operator-approved): an angle piece whose voiced time is mostly a **known off-screen voice** becomes the visible pair's split screen — nobody on screen is framed solo as the speaker, and the piece leaves no framing anchor, so the return to that angle reuses the pre-split crop verbatim — and an interjection shorter than the minimum shot earns a cut **only** when the voice lane confidently attributes it with its speaker on screen in the current angle (structural: an off-screen voice holds no seat, so it can never trigger one); mouth motion alone never does. Each Shot frames its subject from that person's position *during the Shot* (not a whole-clip average), and a solo Shot whose subject **travels** carries a `pan_to` — a bounded *within-shot* follow gliding the same-sized crop so a mover stays framed — but only when the motion is one-way AND too large for one frame to contain (measured gates): wander, a lunge-and-return, and anything a modestly wider frame holds get a **static** frame instead, because a camera in sustained micro-motion over a mostly-still subject reads as jitter; size never changes, group shots never pan. Every plan passes a **camera audit** (camera creep, re-frames without subject cause, a subject riding the crop edge) surfaced in the Camera panel and export summary *before* a render — zero findings on the production fixtures is the regression bar. A printed **face on the set** (a book cover, a poster) is rejected as a track (much smaller than, and far less lively than, the real speakers). Rendered as a per-shot trim/crop concat in one ffmpeg pass (`camera.fg`, a time-expression crop for a follow), captions burned once over the joined stream; trim boundaries print at **full pts precision** — a boundary rounded past its cut frame's pts strands that frame in the outgoing shot's crop, the 1-frame "empty seat" flash at cuts — and the Studio preview picks the crop by the **frame actually on screen** (`display_time`: strip-frame time paused, live-frame midpoint playing, live decode on the source's own fps grid), never the playhead clock, so preview cuts can't flash either (ADR 0038, 2026-07-05). The operator overrides a shot by clicking another face (which drops its follow).
_Avoid_: keyframes (nothing eases; the follow is linear), camera path (the follow is a bounded correction, not free motion), pan-instead-of-cut (speaker changes are always cuts)

**Caption preset**:
A named, complete `CaptionStyle` bundle the Studio's Caption panel starts from — Classic, TikTok, Podcast, Minimal, Gaming, MrBeast — pure data over the extended style fields (outline width/colour, shadow, back box, bold; ADR 0004/0039). Picking one replaces the whole style; every field stays editable after, and any tweak deselects the chip. The Creator store still remembers only the *genre* (ADR 0016).
_Avoid_: theme, template (both suggest something beyond field values)

**Thumbnail intro**:
A still image (JPG/PNG/WebP) shown for the first 0.5–2.0 s (default 1 s) of a Short, AHEAD of the clip (plan #5, ADR 0067). The render concat-prepends it **after** the main stream's ASS burn — the image aspect-fits the 1080×1920 canvas with black bars, gains matched fps/SAR and `anullsrc` silence — so captions, Camera cuts, and razor spans stay source-relative **by construction** (the operator's "everything below should follow"). In the Studio it is a block at Video 1's head occupying output `[0..D]`: the playhead and ruler run in **output time** (`D +` clip), every source-anchored artifact displays at `t + D` through one offset at the strip's t↔x boundary, and time labels show the output clock (they match the exported video). The block's right edge IS its duration control; razor/marker verbs refuse inside it; parked there the canvas shows the ffmpeg-decoded image (the app ships no image decoder) and Play holds it silently before starting audio+video at the crossing. Persisted on the Clip (`thumbnail: { path, duration_s }`) — the ADR 0066 decision-6 moment: track contents becoming project.json data; the image is referenced, never copied.
_Avoid_: cover (that is a platform upload field, not part of the video), intro clip (it is a still, not footage), Images track (deliberately NOT a track — the block lives on Video 1 until a real overlay track arc lands)

**Transcript override**:
The operator's edited transcript from the Studio's caption panel (edit / add / delete / split / merge / censor, `m:ss.cc` timestamps), shipped with a Render and burned **verbatim** — no whisper, no harvest, no silence-drop, no re-timing (ADR 0039): the automated timing machinery exists to clean whisper's guesses, not the operator's words. Per-clip and immediate, unlike Dialect-store curation (durable per Creator, ADR 0031).
_Avoid_: correction (that is the dialect/LLM pass over whisper output), custom captions

**Publish bar**:
The judgment a rendered Clip faces before posting, made by the operator as a publisher: *would I post this?* — a Clip ships when a naive viewer would notice nothing wrong with it. The bar is publisher-honest (ruled 2026-07-07): every defect the operator's eye catches is recorded with its clip-relative timestamp and what the eye saw, even on a Clip that still ships — ship-with-known-wart is allowed and recorded, and that record (not the ship/hold call) is what feeds the next slice.
_Avoid_: gate (a gate is a pre-declared measured bar on an instrument; the publish bar is the operator's publisher eye on a finished Clip), quality check

**Offline**:
The core constraint: all analysis and rendering happens on the local machine. The only permitted network uses are user-initiated: ingesting a VOD, and fetching a missing tool or model from its pinned official source (the Diagnostics page's downloads).
_Avoid_: air-gapped, local-only (both overstate it — ingestion and dependency fetches may use the network)
